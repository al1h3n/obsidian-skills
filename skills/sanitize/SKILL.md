# sanitize

Prepare a privacy-conscious copy of a user-selected note for sharing. Do not transmit or publish it.

## Steps
1. Ask for the source note, intended audience, and whether to abbreviate, flag, or remove personal details. If unclear, flag rather than silently remove.
2. Read only the selected source and necessary context. Identify direct identifiers, contact details, private third-party information, and sensitive specifics.
3. Preserve the note's purpose, factual context, and structure. Replace sensitive details consistently; do not invent substitute facts.
4. Check the result for remaining identifiers and note unresolved risks. Do not claim anonymity if details could re-identify someone.
5. Never edit the original. Write a review copy under `<vault>/AI System/Share/` and show its path. Obtain approval before any external sharing.
